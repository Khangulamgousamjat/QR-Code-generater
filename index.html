Help & Documentation

How to create QR codes:

  1. Select a QR type from the left sidebar (e.g., Personal Details, UPI, Wi-Fi).
  2. Enter any details you want. All personal fields are completely optional.
  3. Click Customize in the preview panel to adjust shapes, dots, colors, or add a logo.
  4. Click Download and choose PNG, JPG, or SVG vector.

Scanning Tips:

Maintain high contrast between foreground and background colors. If embedding a large logo, use High (H) error correction.
